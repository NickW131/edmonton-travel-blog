Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that feel surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with local restaurants, cafés, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • Galaxyland and World Waterpark at the west edmonton mall.
  • Fort Edmonton Park, Heritage park with architechture from 1885, 1905 & 1920's Edmonton.
  • The Alberta Art Galley.
  • The Edmonton Valley Zoo.

Best Time to Visit

There are two time which are the best to vist. The early winter for our winter time events and sports, or come in the summer for our festivals and to enjoy the nature.
